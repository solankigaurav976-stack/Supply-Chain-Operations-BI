# Supply Chain & Operations Business Intelligence System

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end Supply Chain & Operations Business Intelligence project developed using **SQL, Power BI and structured CSV datasets**.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of inventory performance, stock levels, reorder levels and products at risk, helping identify potential inventory and operational issues.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total inventory value and stock levels
- Identify products at risk
- Identify low-stock products
- Compare stock levels against reorder levels
- Analyse inventory risk across product categories
- Provide interactive filtering by category, warehouse, supplier and date
- Support data-driven supply chain and inventory decisions

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualisation
- **SQL / PostgreSQL** – Data management and analysis
- **CSV** – Source datasets
- **Power BI Data Model** – Fact and dimension-based modelling

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- Total Inventory Value
- Total Stock Level
- Products at Risk
- Low Stock Products
- Inventory At Risk %
- Stock Level vs Reorder Level analysis
- Inventory Value At Risk by Category
- Products At Risk by Category
- Category, Warehouse, Supplier and Date filters

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

The dashboard focuses on:

**Inventory Management**
- Stock availability
- Reorder requirements
- Inventory value

**Risk Management**
- Products at risk
- Inventory value at risk
- Category-level risk

**Supply Chain Operations**
- Warehouse analysis
- Supplier analysis
- Inventory trends and operational performance
